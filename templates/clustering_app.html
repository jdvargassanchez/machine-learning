Credit Card Customer Segmentation

Unsupervised Learning · K-Means

About the Dataset

This dataset contains information about approximately 1000 credit card customers, including their financial behavior over the last 6 months. It is commonly used for customer segmentation and marketing strategy analysis.

Each record represents a customer and includes variables related to balance, purchases, payments, and credit usage.

Selected Variables

For this clustering model, only key numerical variables were selected to simplify the analysis and improve interpretability:

  • BALANCE: Remaining account balance
  • PURCHASES: Total purchases made
  • CREDIT_LIMIT: Credit card limit

The variable CUST_ID is used only for identification and is not included in the clustering process.

Model Description

The K-Means algorithm was applied to group customers into three clusters based on their financial behavior. The model identifies patterns without prior labels, allowing automatic segmentation of users.

Application

This segmentation can be used to support business decisions such as:

  • Identifying high-value customers
  • Detecting risky financial behavior
  • Designing personalized marketing strategies
  • Improving credit management policies
