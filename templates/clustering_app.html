Credit Card Customer Segmentation

Unsupervised Learning · K-Means

About the Dataset

This dataset contains information about credit card customers and their financial behavior, including spending patterns, credit limits, and payments.

The objective is to segment customers into groups based on similar financial behavior using unsupervised learning techniques.

Selected Variables

  • CREDIT_LIMIT: Maximum credit available
  • PURCHASES: Total purchases made

These variables were selected because they have a stronger relationship and allow better visualization of customer behavior.

Model Description

K-Means clustering groups customers into 3 clusters based on their financial activity. This allows identifying patterns and segments without predefined labels.
