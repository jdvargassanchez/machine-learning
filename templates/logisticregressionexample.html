Logistic Regression - Health Predictor

How the Model Works

This application uses a Logistic Regression model to predict whether a patient is likely to require hospitalization based on symptoms and medical conditions related to COVID-19.

The model considers factors such as age, gender, vaccination status, symptoms like fever, cough, fatigue, shortness of breath, and pre-existing medical conditions.

It outputs a probability and classifies the patient according to the predicted result.
