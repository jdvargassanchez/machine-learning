Logistic Regression - Health Predictor

How the Model Works

This application uses a Logistic Regression model to predict whether a patient is likely to have COVID-19 based on symptoms and medical conditions, using the dataset "health_dataset.csv for determining the probability of hospitalization of a patient for COVID-19".

The model considers factors such as age, gender, vaccination status, symptoms like fever, cough, fatigue, and pre-existing medical conditions.

It outputs a probability and classifies the patient according to the predicted result.
