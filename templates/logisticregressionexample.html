Logistic Regression - Health Predictor

How the Model Works

This application uses a Logistic Regression model to predict whether a patient is likely to be hospitalized based on their symptoms and medical conditions, based in the dataset "health_dataset.csv for determining the probability of hospitalization of a patient for covid 19".

The model considers factors such as age, gender, vaccination status, and symptoms like fever, cough, and fatigue, as well as pre-existing conditions.

It outputs a probability and classifies the patient as high or low risk.
