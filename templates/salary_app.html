Salary Predictor

Enter years of experience to estimate a salary using Linear Regression.

About this model

This predictor uses Simple Linear Regression, a supervised machine-learning technique that fits a straight line through the training data to model the relationship between one input and one output.

Role Variable Description
Independent (X) experience Years of work experience entered by the user
Dependent (y) salary_usd Estimated annual salary in USD (model output)

The model was trained on train.csv using scikit-learn's LinearRegression. It learns the coefficient (slope) and intercept that minimize the sum of squared errors between the predicted and actual salaries in the dataset.
