Smart Health Colombia

Supervised Learning · Binary Classification

Problem Description

In Colombia, many people discover cardiovascular issues when it is already too late. The current healthcare system often acts reactively, especially in rural areas where access to specialists is limited. This delay leads to late diagnoses, more expensive treatments, and avoidable severe consequences for patients.

Type of Machine Learning

This project is based on Supervised Learning. The model is trained with historical medical datasets to categorize patients into specific risk levels. Specifically, it uses a Binary Classification algorithm to determine if a patient is at high risk of a cardiovascular event or not.

Input Data

The system analyzes basic and clinical patient information such as:

  • Biometrics: Age, Gender, and Body Mass Index (BMI).
  • Clinical Vitals: Blood pressure and heart rate.
  • Lab Results: Cholesterol levels (LDL/HDL) and glucose.
  • Lifestyle: Smoking habits, physical activity, and medical history.

Expected Output

The expected output is a probability score of risk. If the model identifies a high probability of heart disease, it triggers a preventive alert: "High cardiovascular risk detected. Preventive medical evaluation is highly recommended."

Real-World Relevance

This application is crucial for transforming healthcare in Colombia from reactive to preventive. By implementing this tool, the health system can reduce hospitalizations, optimize medical resources, and, most importantly, save lives through early detection and timely intervention.
